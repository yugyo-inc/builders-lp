The Yame Protocol.

Yame's chapter is built around the region's living craft economy — tea fields, indigo and textile studios, a centuries-old sake brewery, and satoyama terraces still worked by hand. Mornings are yours to choose between hands-on experiences; the town opens up around you the rest of the day.

Base Camp: Yame Green Hotel.

Right in the center of Yame City, an old post town where white-walled streets still stand dotted with traditional craft shops, museums, restaurants and general stores.

Single Room (Non-smoking)

Simple, comfortable single rooms, each with its own desk set along the wall — everything you need after a day out in the tea fields, nothing you don't. The hotel sits right in the old post town itself, so the white-walled streets, craft shops and restaurants you'll be exploring are a two-minute walk from your door.

where you'll stay

Base Camp: HOTEL R9 The Yard.

HOTEL R9 The Yard is something you don't see often — private rooms built out of shipping containers. The exterior looks the part, but inside it's comfortable enough that you'll forget you're staying in a container at all.

Accommodation partner

HOTEL R9 The Yard

Double Room (Non-Smoking) · container hotel

Every room has a Simmons double bed (W140 × L195), a desk, a chair, and Wi-Fi — a private space with what you need to actually unwind after a full day. It's more comfortable than you'd expect from a container: not camping, just a small, well laid-out room built for getting work done and then resting.
